# GeoWake — Consolidated Gap Analysis vs the Core Promise

_Merges five verification lenses (never-late chain, GPS-loss underground, Android kill/Doze/OEM, metro-data correctness, real-ride scenarios) and the 18 subsystem maps into one deduplicated, ranked report. Core promise: **wake a transit rider before their stop — never late, never at the wrong place — even when GPS dies underground, on a cheap Android phone, in India.** File:line references were verified against source by the lenses this session._

## 2. Triage: ship-blockers vs fix-soon vs monitor

### Ship-blockers (the promise is broken for a whole rider class or unmeasurable — do these before any "works" claim)
- **Arm-time honesty:** enforce the preflight `block` verdict (notifications/DND/exact-alarm) and stop refusing the interstate sleeper. GeoWake must either guarantee a fireable delivery channel or honestly refuse/degrade — never arm silently on a dead channel.
- **Make the never-late net actually run and cover every mode:** drive reachability from a wall-clock tick that never early-returns; seed the anchor at arm time from the first real on-route fix (not `s=0` at origin); feed the reach bound / a symmetric ETA lower-bound into distance, non-metro time, non-metro 60%, and geofence paths.
- **A trustworthy, mode-accurate, process-death-surviving backstop:** encode the real stops/distance lead (not 60 s), re-derive from the reach bound, cancel/re-arm it consistently on every path, and fix Android-14+ boot/watchdog resume (FGS split + custom boot receiver).
- **Measure it:** persist telemetry (`on-time vs late`, `OS-killed/Doze/backstop-fired`) and put the never-late replay gate in CI with committed fixtures and never-early/wrong-place assertions. Bump `versionCode` from the hardcoded `1` so a fix can actually ship.

### Fix-soon (high real-world harm, narrower blast radius)
- `V_LINE` city plumbing + unknown→`absoluteCeilingMps` (fast-line late-fire).
- Route-geometry-independent dead-reckoning + compact persisted polyline (frozen-progress and route-less recovery).
- DND policy-access + Android-14 full-screen-intent request paths; OEM `_applyFix` deep-links + verification.
- Offline route pinning (TTL exemption); reroute whitelist for commuter/tram/light-rail; audible wrong-direction alert.
- ETA σ floor underground + wire the scheduled metro ETA model.
- Runtime metro-data validation gate; ordered sequences for the 9 flagged lines + Gurugram; `stopCountConfidence` consumed.
- Server attestation + `response.data.status` handling + app-side route fallback.

### Monitor (correct-direction, hygiene, or gated by an earlier fix)
- Watchdog `hardTMaxSeconds`/topology cap arming; power-tier re-evaluation; arm-race ordering; equirectangular snapping; city-vote → known-city; reroute latency band; `RouteLogger` privacy; APK bloat; R8 keep-rules; `USE_EXACT_ALARM` policy; monetization wiring (no alarm impact).

## 3. Verdict — does GeoWake deliver its core promise end to end today?

**Partly — and only on one narrow, happy path.** The genuine achievement is real: the reachability Protection Level is a sound, unit-proven, physics-based never-late lever, wired into the fire decision, and validated on real GPS-dead ride data in the offline replay harness. For a **metro-stops-mode** rider who (a) gets at least one real GPS fix before the tunnel, (b) has route geometry registered, (c) is on a line whose `lineName` keyword-resolves a correct `V_LINE`, (d) has notifications on and DND off, and (e) is on a phone that doesn't force-kill the isolate — the app wakes them before the stop, late-proof by physics. That is more than most competitors can honestly claim.

But end to end, the promise has holes at every stage, and the holes cluster in exactly the conditions the promise names. **Underground:** the never-late evaluation early-returns unless a fix already arrived and an EKF exists (BLOCK), and the anchor is seeded `s=0` at the route origin, so a rider who boards already-underground is anchored kilometres behind reality (BLOCK). **Never-late as an app-wide property:** false — the physics bound only covers metro-stops mode; distance, non-metro time, non-metro 60%, and geofence have no reachability backstop (BLOCK). **Cheap Android in India:** boot/watchdog resume is broken-by-construction on Android 14+, OEM/battery survival is probabilistic and unverified, DND and full-screen wake are unhandled, and the sole surviving net after a kill — the OS backstop — is mistimed for stops/distance (60 s flat), can be left armed after "End Tracking," and is never scheduled at all if the rider armed with a misconfigured delivery channel. **Wrong place:** fast RRTS lines can fire late from a too-low `V_LINE`; whole cities fall to uniform stop guesses; the wrong-train warning is a silent banner. **The flagship trip:** the interstate overnight sleeper is refused at the door.

And the deepest problem is not any single gap — it is that **the app cheerfully arms alarms it cannot deliver, and records nothing when it misses.** The preflight `block` verdict is a dismissible tooltip; the never-late gate isn't in CI so the guarantee is a one-time local observation; the telemetry funnel that would tell you which phones fail to wake their riders has zero emit sites and evaporates on the exact kill it exists to measure. Until arming either guarantees a fireable, mode-accurate, process-death-surviving channel or honestly degrades — and until the app measures on-time-vs-late outcomes in the field and gates never-late in CI — GeoWake does **not** yet deliver its core promise end to end for the rider it was built for. The physics is honest and the path exists; the surrounding contract that would make it trustworthy on a real Indian commute does not yet close.
