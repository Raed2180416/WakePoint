The core invariant

Never silently late.

The single rule that governs every decision in GeoWake. If the alarm can't fire on time, it doesn't fire at all.

How it works — physics, not guesswork

While GPS is lost (in a tunnel), the train can't be further along the route than the physical speed limit allows. GeoWake computes an upper bound on your progress — the moment it's physically possible you're near your stop, the alarm fires.

s_max(t) = s₀ + V_LINE × (t − t₀)

Because the estimate is always an over-approximation, the alarm may fire early — but never late.

  • Anchor is a real GPS fix, never a phantom
  • Speed ceiling ≥ line's true maximum (28 m/s metro, 56 m/s RRTS)
  • Tuned for precision: missed detections only relax the bound upward (safe)
  • CI-gated by a never-late replay harness on every commit
