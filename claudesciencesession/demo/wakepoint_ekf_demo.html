What you're seeing

Underground (grey band) the GPS signal is gone, so the filter must dead-reckon on IMU alone. The production filter's motion detector mistakes the smooth cruise for a standstill and repeatedly zeroes its velocity (the "spurious ZUPTs" counter) — so it stalls, drifts far behind the true position, and only lurches forward when GPS returns. By then the target stop is long past → alarm fires ~200 s late = missed stop. The fixed filter fuses a phone-independent learned velocity estimate every 0.5 s, so it keeps moving at the right speed through the blackout, and fires the wake alarm on time.
