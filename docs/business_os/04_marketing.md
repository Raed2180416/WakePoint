# Business OS — Zero-Budget Marketing

Free-only, solo-dev, India-first. Full sourced version in
`research/marketing_playbook_raw.md`. This is the committed plan + what the
autopilot can and cannot do here.

## 1. Hard rules (autonomy boundary)

Marketing content is DRAFTED by agents, PUBLISHED by you (governance L3-only).
Never do (policy/legal risk, verified):
- Incentivized/exchanged reviews ("rate-for-rate") — banned, GenAI-detected at
  scale in 2025.
- Unauthorized QR posters on metro/station property — real fine + imprisonment
  under state Defacement Acts (Maharashtra: up to 3mo/₹2,000; verify per state).
- Identical cross-posts across city subreddits — reads as spam, gets you banned
  from the channel permanently.

## 2. The channels that actually work (prioritized)

1. **ASO (compounds everything).** Title ≤30 chars, short desc ≤80 chars
   (verified Play caps). Localize listing to Hindi + launch-city language.
   Wire the in-app review prompt to fire **only after a successful wake** —
   policy-safe review velocity at the moment of peak delight.
2. **POV-format Reels / YouTube Shorts (highest zero-cost leverage).** "Fell
   asleep on the metro, GeoWake woke me at my stop" realism format. Iterate
   weekly on hook rate. This is the single best free acquisition channel per
   2026 data.
3. **Founder-story post in the launch-city subreddit** (r/bangalore etc.) —
   "I built this because I kept missing my stop", disclose dev status, verify
   each sub's self-promo rules live before posting. One per sub, never identical.
4. **Journey-share viral loop.** Every shared journey link is an impression for
   someone who rides the same route. Add a "Get GeoWake — never miss your stop"
   CTA to the share landing page. This is the compounding growth engine and it's
   already half-built (share backend live).
5. **PR (low hit-rate, high value):** MediaNama (privacy/data-pipeline angle is
   the strongest fit), YourStory, Inc42, OfficeChai — 4 tailored pitches, not a
   mass email.
6. **Campus ambassadors** (certificate + Pro unlock, not cash) and city
   commuter Telegram/WhatsApp groups — slow-burn trust, not broadcast.

## 3. Social proof we already have (verified)

A BMRCL official-app review (377 upvotes, 2020) explicitly begs for a
train-timing/alarm feature that still doesn't exist in 2026. Use it: "even riders
on the official metro app have been asking for this for years."

## 4. Where the autopilot fits

- **Drafts** (agents, you approve+send): Reel scripts, subreddit posts, PR
  pitches, review replies, store copy + localizations.
- **Monitors** (agents, autonomous): review sentiment classification →
  bug-reviews become engineering tasks (see `02`); competitor rating/complaint
  tracking; ASO keyword rank drift.
- **Never autonomous:** anything published under the brand.

## 5. 90-day shape

- Days 1–14: ASO + localization + review-prompt wiring + film 3 Reels.
- Days 15–35: soft launch, seed launch-city subreddit + Telegram groups, first
  Reels batch, 4 PR pitches.
- Days 36–60: double down on whatever drove installs (per-city custom listing
  deep links give free attribution), campus ambassadors, ship/tune the share
  viral loop.
- Days 61–90: reallocate to the winning channel/format on real data;
  second-city listing if traction.

Success is measured by installs → **successful-wake retention**, not vanity
downloads. A user the alarm failed once is churned and leaves a 1-star review;
product reliability (`01`) is the real growth lever.
