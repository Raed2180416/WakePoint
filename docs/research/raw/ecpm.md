# India mobile in-app ad monetization 2026 — realistic eCPM, fill rates, mediation, and rewarded-vs-interstitial reality for GeoWake (Android-first India transit utility app), for a break-even model

## Summary
For an Android-first utility app in India, realistic BLENDED in-app eCPM in 2026 is roughly $0.40–$1.50 (MonetizeMore, Jan 2026) — an order of magnitude below the US ($5–12). Per-format India-Android reality: banner ~$0.10–0.30, native ~$0.50–1.50, interstitial ~$1.00–2.50, rewarded video ~$1.50–4.00 (occasionally to $5–6 with strong mediation). Do NOT plug APAC regional averages (e.g. rewarded ~$8.20 Android) into an India model — those are inflated by Japan/Korea/Australia. India eCPMs broadly DECLINED through 2025 (Android banner −31.7% Q1→Q2 2025), with Android rewarded video the only format that grew. Rewarded pays ~2–4x interstitial per impression, but a brief-session wake-alarm is a poor fit for a 15–30s opt-in video, so interstitial at natural transitions is more realistic — and both formats are volume-capped by how rarely a transit alarm is opened. Net for the break-even model: expect roughly $0.02–$0.18 of ad revenue per DAU per month in India (derived), so break-even is DAU-driven, not eCPM-driven; mediation (AdMob bidding + AppLovin + Unity + InMobi, plus Meta Audience Network via bidding) mainly protects fill rate (~90–98% for banner/interstitial, lower for rewarded) rather than lifting India eCPM into tier-1 territory.

## Caveats
- DERIVED break-even reality: at India blended eCPM $0.40–$1.50 and a wake-alarm's low impression count (~2–4 impressions per active user per day), ad revenue is roughly $0.0008–$0.006 per DAU/day ~= $0.02–$0.18 per DAU/MONTH. Break-even is therefore driven by DAU scale and impressions-per-session, not by squeezing eCPM. Validate this arithmetic against your own funnel before trusting any revenue line.
- APAC/regional averages (e.g. rewarded ~$8.20 Android) are inflated by Japan/Korea/Australia and OVERSTATE India by ~4–8x for premium formats — the single biggest modeling trap here. Use India-specific low-end figures.
- Almost all public eCPM benchmarks are self-interested vendor/mediation blogs (MonetizeMore, Playwire, Appodeal, Unity, AppLovin). The only authoritative India per-format number is your OWN AdMob dashboard once live; treat everything here as a planning band, not a promise.
- Primary rate cards were partly inaccessible this session: Business of Apps CPM research page returned HTTP 403, and Appodeal/AppLovin/TopOn full reports are gated PDFs/downloads. India per-format figures are triangulated from secondary sources, not a single primary dataset.
- Gaming-app benchmarks dominate this space and overstate a utility app; conversely a wake-alarm has near-zero natural rewarded-video moments, so rewarded's headline eCPM advantage is largely unrealizable unless you design a genuine value-exchange (e.g. watch-to-unlock premium tones / offline route packs / 24h ad-free).
- India eCPMs were falling, not rising, through 2025 and are seasonal (dip around the March fiscal-year-end and Q2 school holidays) — model a declining/soft trend, not growth, and do not annualize a peak month.
- Google policy constrains the realistic formats: interstitials at app-open and after-alarm must respect frequency caps and the disruptive-/accidental-click policies, and app-open ads have their own restrictions — aggressive interstitial stacking risks policy strikes on a utility app.

## Open questions
- What is GeoWake's realistic impressions-per-DAU given the app is opened briefly ~1–2x per trip and runs mostly in the background? This, not eCPM, dominates the revenue line and is unknown from external data.
- Is there a genuine, non-annoying rewarded value-exchange for a transit wake-alarm (unlock premium ringtones, offline maps/route packs, remove-ads-for-a-day)? Without one, rewarded's higher eCPM is unrealizable and the model should lean on interstitial + banner.
- What DAU is required to break even against server/Maps/Play/OEM-testing costs at $0.02–$0.18 ad revenue per DAU/month? A subscription or one-time-unlock IAP tier may be mathematically necessary alongside ads.
- Actual live fill rate and eCPM in YOUR mediation stack (AdMob bidding + AppLovin + Unity + InMobi + Meta AN) in India — only measurable post-integration via a small live cohort or A/B; external India-specific fill numbers were not published in any source found.
- Does the current Google Mobile Ads / AdMob SDK version and India data-consent (DPDP Act) posture affect fill or require a consent/UMP flow that suppresses eCPM? Not covered by monetization benchmarks and worth a separate compliance check.
