# Authoritative ordered station sequences for high-ridership Delhi/NCR + Gurugram metro lines (Blue incl. Vaishali/Noida branch, Yellow, Red, Violet, Pink, Magenta, Green, Rapid Metro Gurgaon) — to seed corrected sequence data for GeoWake, with 2024–2026 extensions/renames flagged.

## Summary
Ordered terminus-to-terminus station sequences were compiled and cross-checked (primarily English Wikipedia line articles, triangulated with YoMetro and DMRC/news sources for recent changes). Order is stable and well-attested for Blue, Yellow, Red, Violet, and Green. Four material changes in the last 18 months affect sequences and names: (1) Pink Line ring closure — the Majlis Park↔Maujpur-Babarpur arc opened 8 March 2026, making it India's first ring/circular metro line (~46 stations incl. Shiv Vihar branch; Soorghat built but bypassed/not operational); (2) Magenta Line western terminus extended one stop to Krishna Park Extension (opened 5 Jan 2025, initially a shuttle beyond Janakpuri West); (3) Udyog Bhawan on the Yellow Line renamed Seva Teerth (Feb 2026); (4) HUDA City Centre renamed Millennium City Centre Gurugram (2023). Blue Line remains Dwarka Sector 21↔Noida Electronic City (50) with the Line-4 branch diverging at Yamuna Bank to Vaishali (8 stns). Rapid Metro Gurgaon is an 11-station horseshoe with a one-directional Cyber City loop, interchanging the Yellow Line at Sikanderpur. Under-construction Phase-4 interchanges (Magenta at several stns, Golden Line, etc.) are NOT yet operational and should not be treated as live transfers. Each line's full ordered list is embedded in the corresponding keyFact 'value'.

## Caveats
- Primary source for the ordered sequences was English Wikipedia line articles fetched this session (they carry maintained station tables), triangulated with YoMetro and DMRC/news for the recent changes. These are not the DMRC operator site directly (dmrc.metrorailindia.co.in was not fetched this session) — for the final production seed, do one confirmation pass against the official DMRC network map/station list, especially for spellings.
- WebFetch uses a small summarizer model that occasionally garbles/blends names. Two spots to re-verify before ingest: (a) Red Line stations 2-4 (Rithala->Rohini West->Rohini East->Pitampura->Kohat Enclave is the canonical order; the fetch returned 'Dr Baba Saheb Ambedkar Hospital/Rohini/Madhuban Chowk' which conflates a Phase-4 UC station); (b) Pink Line new-arc station names have spelling variants across sources (Sonia Vihar vs Nanaksar-Sonia Vihar; Jagatpur vs Jagatpur-Wazirabad).
- Under-construction Phase-4 interchanges are NOT live transfers and must not be encoded as active in GeoWake: e.g. Magenta interchanges shown at Haiderpur Badli Mor, Azadpur, Ramakrishna Ashram Marg, Pulbangash, Delhi Gate, Indraprastha; Golden Line (Line 10, Aerocity-Tughlakabad) interchanges at Chhatarpur, Lajpat Nagar, Tughlakabad; these are future.
- Pink Line Soorghat station is physically built but bypassed (not operational) due to a DDA land dispute — keep it in the geometry but exclude it as a stoppable/wake target until it opens.
- Magenta Line: at the 5 Jan 2025 launch, Krishna Park Extension<->Janakpuri West ran as an isolated shuttle separate from the Botanical Garden<->Janakpuri West trunk. Whether through-running has since been unified was not definitively confirmed this session — treat Krishna Park Extension as a new terminus but verify service continuity before assuming a single unbroken ride from Botanical Garden.
- Rapid Metro Gurgaon's Cyber City loop is one-directional; a rider going 'north' physically passes the Cyber City-side stations in loop order, not as a simple there-and-back. GeoWake's directionality/ETA logic for this line must model the loop, not a bidirectional segment.
- US-only web search and 15-min WebFetch caching were in effect; a few figures (line lengths, total counts) are secondary and were not the focus — ORDER was prioritized as instructed.
